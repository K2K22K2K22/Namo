# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making Namo a welcoming, respectful, and collaborative open-source project. We want everyone, regardless of their background, identity, experience level, or personal characteristics, to be able to participate without fear of harassment or discrimination.

All contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to act professionally and treat one another with respect.

## Expected Behaviour

Examples of behaviour that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful, considerate, and constructive in discussions and reviews.
- Giving and accepting feedback professionally.
- Focusing criticism on ideas, code, and technical decisions rather than individuals.
- Respecting different perspectives, experiences, and levels of expertise.
- Using inclusive language and communicating in good faith.
- Accepting responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Helping maintain a collaborative environment in which people can learn and contribute.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviours are not acceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory, hateful, or degrading language or conduct.
- Sexual harassment or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Deliberate trolling, persistent disruption, or abusive communication.
- Publishing another person's private information without their permission.
- Deliberately misrepresenting another contributor's work or contributions.
- Other conduct that is reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional open-source community.

Disagreement about code, project direction, design decisions, or technical approaches is acceptable. Personal hostility and abusive conduct are not.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all Namo project spaces, including:

- GitHub issues and pull requests.
- Code reviews and project discussions.
- Project-related communication channels and community spaces.
- Public interactions where someone is representing Namo or its community.

Maintainers may also consider conduct outside these spaces when it has a direct and demonstrable impact on the safety or well-being of the project community.

## Responsibilities of Maintainers

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards described in this document.

Maintainers may remove or edit comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. They may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate to the circumstances, based on available information, and handled as consistently as possible.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it privately to the project maintainers.

**Reporting contact:** herogupta626@gmail.com

Please include relevant details, such as links to discussions, screenshots where appropriate, and a description of what happened. Share only information necessary to explain the incident.

Maintainers will review reports as promptly as reasonably possible and handle them with discretion. Information will be shared only with those who need it to assess or address the report, where practicable.

Please do not submit sensitive reports as public GitHub issues.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take one or more of the following actions depending on the severity and circumstances of a violation:

1. **Warning:** A private or public warning explaining the behaviour that must stop.
2. **Temporary restriction:** A temporary restriction on participation in specific project spaces.
3. **Removal:** Removal of offending content or contributions.
4. **Temporary suspension:** A temporary suspension from project participation.
5. **Permanent ban:** Permanent exclusion from project spaces and community participation.

Maintainers may skip intermediate steps when a serious violation warrants stronger action.

Retaliation against someone for reporting a concern in good faith, or for participating in an investigation, is itself a violation of this Code of Conduct.
